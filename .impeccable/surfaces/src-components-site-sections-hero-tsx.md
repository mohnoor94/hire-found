# Hero - surface brief
Scope: homepage first viewport. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: employers first, then talk/workshop bookers; candidates exit to vacancies.
Job: in seconds, visitor knows this is Yasmin's founder-led practice, feels the voice, and books or explores roles.
Proof: panel photograph, TEDx and MENA credentials, live-role path.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage opens like a magazine cover, not a brochure. The verse is the cover line and Yasmin on stage is the cover image; everything else is masthead and caption.
OWN-WORLD: Linen ground, burgundy DM Serif display, gold rule and frame offset, solid burgundy caption chip on the photo. No gradients, no glow, no glass.
STORY: Read the promise, see the person who keeps it, act through one burgundy pill or the quiet roles link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop splits 55/45. Left: HireFound wordmark small at top, oversized two-line H1, second stanza, support line, CTA row. Right: tall panel photo with gold offset frame and caption chip. Proof strip rules the bottom. Mobile stacks type, CTA, photo, proof.
FORM: Editorial cover, user-selected from three directions (code-led).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# HireFound - Product Context

## What this is
HireFound is the boutique executive search and recruitment practice of Yasmin Blasi, based in Jordan and serving the MENA region. The public site at hirefound.com introduces the practice, lists live vacancies, and routes employers, event organisers, and early-career audiences to a single booking conversation.

## Who it serves
- Employers hiring from junior to C-suite who want direct founder access instead of an agency handoff.
- Event and university partners booking talks, workshops, and sessions for people starting out.
- Candidates discovering open roles through the vacancies board. Candidates are not sold coaching or CV services.

## Product truth
- Founder-led: every engagement is with Yasmin directly. 10+ years in HR and recruitment.
- Positioning: matchmaking, not seat-filling. Fit and story over keywords.
- Proof on the page: TEDx speaker, panel and summit photography, live vacancies, and the booking calendar.
- One conversion door: the booking dialog (Let's Talk). Secondary path: Explore open roles.

## Brand commitments
- Warm linen ground, burgundy primary, gold secondary. DM Serif display with Inter body.
- Voice is plain, warm, and first-person. Short lines. No hype, no agency-speak.
- Yasmin's photography is a trust asset and should lead where possible.

## Constraints
- Static Next.js export. No new backend for marketing surfaces.
- Accessibility: visible focus, reduced-motion support, 44px minimum touch targets.
- Copy rule: no em dashes anywhere. Use hyphens, commas, or sentence breaks.
